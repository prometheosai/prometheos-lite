## Existing work / reconciliation
`src/harness/failure.rs` has a typed failure and recovery taxonomy; #133 already specifies immutable effective ExecutionProfile and CompatibilityDecision; #155 already owns checkpoint portability; #156 owns replayable failure fixtures; #334 owns governed evaluations. Historically `src/flow/idempotency.rs::compute_key` concatenated three strings with colons and hashed with DefaultHasher, so distinct triples could form identical preimages (e.g. `a:b,c,d` and `a,b:c,d`). That source-observed encoding defect was repaired by merged PR #359 at `8b6df9c` using a versioned canonical encoding, a stable digest, and legacy completed-receipt read compatibility; it is not reopened here. Atomic outbox admission and checked completion were subsequently implemented by merged PR #363 at main `a2bf5e44907c7947d07483ed99842cdd9bf01848` (merged head `43b3b0cd7e9439ae9e4d3467b15414c1c841129c`); its `tests/outbox_atomic_admission.rs` proves competing canonical claims, canonical-versus-legacy races, immutable completed receipts, malformed persisted timestamps and dual-version admission. Those merged cases are evidence for those criteria, not full #343 acceptance. No production duplicate was proven. Existing journal/authority owners remain canonical.

- [#343](https://github.com/prometheosai/prometheos-lite/issues/343): COMPLETED by merged PR #359 — ambiguous tuple encoding in `IdempotencyKey::compute_key` was replaced by a versioned canonical preimage and stable digest; legacy completed receipts remain readable; RED/GREEN distinct-tuple, version, empty/delimiter, JSON key-order and legacy-compatibility tests were published. MERGED #363 evidence: competing canonical claims, canonical-versus-legacy claim race, completed receipt immutability, malformed persisted timestamp refusal and dual-version admission refusal are implemented in `tests/outbox_atomic_admission.rs`. PROPOSED REMAINING criteria under #343 (not claimed complete): duplicate pending rows, pending-versus-completed receipt distinction beyond the covered overwrite case, zero-row completion evidence, crash after effect before acknowledgement, and uncertain replay with reconnect. Hash alone is not atomic admission, and merged #363 does not close #343 as a whole. Do not alter unrelated journal/authority semantics.

Include exact model/harness/tools/environment/profile and independent verifier identity; stale positive and negative outcomes do not transfer automatically. Test identical error text with different trusted cause, unknown attribution, and no automatic escalation on non-model failure.
- [#334](https://github.com/prometheosai/prometheos-lite/issues/334): In existing governed evaluation receipts, compare first-pass independently accepted work and total bounded resource cost per accepted task, including failed attempts and verification. For zero accepted tasks report undefined/no accepted result, not zero cost. Add deterministic task-class/difficulty/risk fixtures with explicit provenance; labels are metadata, not capability or permission. Compare fixed baseline with an offline hypothetical escalation ladder only, no live policy activation.
